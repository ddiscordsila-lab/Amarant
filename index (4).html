Our Heritage

Crafting Unforgettable Moments
Since 2018

Amarant was born out of a desire to create an oasis where culinary passion meets refined aesthetic. Named after the immortal amaranth flower, our kitchen honors enduring culinary traditions while continually innovating for modern connoisseurs.

Every dish is curated using sustainably sourced, organic ingredients transformed by our world-class team of chefs led by Executive Chef Marcus Vance.
